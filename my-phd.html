Awareness of unawareness

Uncertainty: exploring the current hypotheses inside your hypothesis set.

ℋt = {h₁, h₂, h₃}

Unawareness: the feeling that the correct hypothesis h* is not in the set at all.

P(h* ∉ ℋt) > 0

What does the agent do next?

We frame this not as search but as an information need: keep seeking information vs commit.

What alternative explanations matter?

Anything else that could cause the same information-seeking behavior without awareness of unawareness: ordinary uncertainty, low confidence, noise, missing evidence, misunderstanding, random exploration, a generic "always reconsider" strategy, search-cost differences.

Sequential decision-making

A decision problem is sequential when the agent does not choose only once; the agent faces later decision points whose circumstances may depend on earlier choices, information, or changes in preference.
